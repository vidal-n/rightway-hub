Privacy

Rightway Growth LLC is a local marketing agency in Miami and Fort Lauderdale, Florida. This page explains what we do with the information our clients and leads give us, including what we receive when you connect Facebook or Instagram.

Facebook and Instagram

When you sign in with Facebook through a link we send you, you choose which Pages, Instagram accounts and ad accounts to share. Facebook gives us a sign-in token and the names and ids of what you shared. We use that access only to publish posts, read results for your reports, reply to comments and manage ads for your business, when you have asked us to. We never see your Facebook password.

We store the token encrypted. We do not sell it, share it with other businesses or use it for anything outside the work you hired us for.

Removing access and deleting your data

You can remove our access any time in Facebook under Settings, Business integrations. When you do, Facebook tells us and we delete the stored token and the list of accounts. To have everything we hold about you deleted, use Facebook's delete request for the app, or email us at the address below. We confirm once it is done.

Other information

We keep the business details, contact details, call and text records, files and reports needed to serve you. Calls and texts follow the consent and do-not-call rules that apply. We do not sell personal information.

Contact

Rightway Growth LLC, Miami, Florida. Questions or deletion requests: reply to any email from us, or write to the address on our website at rightwaygrowth.com.
